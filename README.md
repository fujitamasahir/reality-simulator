# Reality Simulator

**Experience anything. Learn by doing. Prove your skills.**

[Try the web demo](https://fujitamasahir.github.io/reality-simulator/)

People learn through work, experimentation, failure, and interaction. Yet the experience they need to grow does not always occur at the right time.

Reality Simulator turns learning into interactive, fictional workplaces. Investigate evidence, take action, observe consequences, and develop practical skills. The live AI mode is designed to generate a workplace from any topic or learning material, evaluate free-text actions, and adapt the next experience to the learner's demonstrated skills.

Traditional adaptive learning optimizes which question a learner should answer next. Reality Simulator aims to optimize which **experience** a learner should have next.

Our long-term goal is to maximize practical knowledge gained per unit of time and issue verifiable skill certificates that help learners show their abilities to employers.

**Status:** curated robotics and security demos are available; the live generative mode requires a separately deployed AI backend. AI evaluations are unvalidated and are not credentials.
